How onsite massage works in St. Maarten

The complete guide.

An onsite massage in St. Maarten is a professional treatment at the exact spot you choose. That could be a villa terrace, your hotel room, an Airbnb, a yacht deck or the beach. A licensed therapist arrives with a padded table, fresh linens and oils. A 60-minute relaxation massage starts at $105: an hour of serenity without leaving your stay.

What to expect

This guide explains how the service works, what each treatment costs, and how to book it, including on the same day.

Onsite massage reverses the usual spa arrangement. You choose the spot, and we bring the whole treatment there. So there's no driving, no waiting room and no locker room. We work this way across the whole island, on both the Dutch and French sides. So a villa in Orient Bay gets the same service as a condo in Simpson Bay.

Every listed price includes the onsite setup anywhere on the island, the table, linens, towels and oils. There are no booking fees. The service runs from 7:30am to 8pm every day. Payment is cash only, in US dollars, handed to your therapist after the massage rather than before.

Who the service suits

The same team handles very different settings. Each one has a few practical details worth knowing in advance.

  • Couples

    A couples massage uses two therapists on two tables side by side. That way you both enjoy your massage at the same time. The 60-minute session is $210 and the 90-minute version is $315. Most people book it for an anniversary or a honeymoon evening, on a pool deck or covered terrace. Plus, you skip the drive and the spa reception. More about couples massage.

  • Hotel guests

    In-room hotel massage is $105 for 60 minutes, and the therapist comes up to your room. Tell the front desk that a therapist is on the way. Hotels in Maho and other resort areas often route visitors through reception, so a quick call ahead prevents a wait in the lobby.

  • Villa and Airbnb renters

    Rentals are the most common setting. The therapist can work in a living room, on a terrace or beside the pool. Also, villa massage in St. Martin costs the same as on the Dutch side, from $105. Check your rental's house rules on outside visitors if you are unsure. More about Airbnb and villa massage.

  • Groups and bachelorettes

    Group bookings cover 4 to 9 people at the same villa for $95 each, and ten or more guests pay $90 each. Therapists arrive in pairs, so tables run side by side and no one waits alone at the end. That's why groups need to book early. For a birthday, the $250 package covers the guest of honor and one friend with two therapists and a bottle of champagne. More about group massage.

  • Yacht crews and guests

    Yacht massage takes place on board, either at a dock or at anchor, from $105. Crew members can book too, not only charter guests. In fact, four or more crew on the same boat pay the group rate of $95 each. The table needs a flat stretch of deck, and a calm anchorage makes for a steadier session than an exposed one. More about yacht massage.

What you'll need before booking

Have these details ready before you send a message. They cover everything the booking team asks.

  • Your exact location: villa name, hotel and room number, rental address, or marina and berth.
  • The number of people.
  • Your preferred date and start time, keeping in mind the 7:30am to 8pm window.
  • The treatment you want for each person, and whether you'd like a male therapist.
  • Enough US cash to cover the full amount, plus $20 for any session starting after 7pm.
  • Space for the table, indoors or outdoors. One table needs about 2 x 3 meters (6 x 10 feet), and a couples massage about 3 x 3 meters (10 x 10 feet).

The therapist brings the rest

A professional padded table, fresh sheets and towels for each guest, massage oils (unscented on request), and quiet music if you want it.

How to book, step by step

Most bookings take a few messages, and many are arranged for the same day.

  1. Message on WhatsApp

    Send your details to +1-721-554-0065. WhatsApp is the quickest channel, especially for same-day requests, though phone calls are also accepted. Put all your details in the first message. If you'd like a male therapist, say so here. The team is mainly female, so we need to arrange one in advance. A message that only says “Are you available?” adds a round trip before we can schedule anything.

  2. Receive confirmation

    The team confirms your therapist and arrival time, usually quickly during the day, depending on therapist availability.

  3. Prepare the space

    Clear the area, move breakable items, and switch off ceiling fans if you get cold easily. For hotel bookings, call the front desk now. For yachts, share the dock or anchorage location clearly.

  4. Let the therapist set up

    Setup takes about 10 minutes. For example, if you book a 6pm session, the table will be ready a little after the therapist arrives. So plan dinner around that.

  5. Relax, then pay

    After the session, pay your therapist in US dollars. We don't accept cards. So if your villa is far from an ATM, withdraw cash before the day of your appointment.

Choosing your therapist

All therapists are licensed and most are female, with experience in resorts, spas and private vacation homes. A male therapist is available on request. Some guests prefer a male therapist for deep tissue work, and some prefer a female therapist for any treatment. Both are reasonable, and the only requirement is that you ask when you book.

Our therapists have already set up in many villas, hotel rooms and boat decks around the island. So the person at your door knows the routine.

Common mistakes and how to avoid them

A few problems come up repeatedly, and each has a simple fix.

  • Booking a group too late

    A party of eight needs several therapists free at the same time. Book as early as your plans allow, especially for weekend bachelorette trips.

  • Forgetting the evening surcharge

    A session that starts at 7:15pm costs $20 more. Start before 7pm if you want the standard rate.

  • Arriving without cash

    There is no card option. Count your US dollars before the therapist arrives.

  • Not telling the hotel

    Front desk staff who are not expecting a visitor can delay entry by 10 or 15 minutes, which cuts into your session.

  • Using the wrong contact

    Imitation services exist. SXM Onsite Massage only takes bookings through sxm-massage-onsite.com and +1-721-554-0065. A different number or website is not this company. How to check.

How often to book during a vacation

No fixed rule applies, and the right frequency depends on your trip. Many visitors book once to recover from travel and again near the end of the stay. If you are hiking, snorkeling or sailing every day, a deep tissue session mid-trip addresses the soreness those activities cause. Because same-day booking is available, you can decide as the week goes on rather than committing on arrival.
