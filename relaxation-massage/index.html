The treatment

Relaxation massage is what spas often call Swedish massage. Your therapist uses long, smooth strokes with light to medium pressure to ease tired muscles and slow your breathing down.

We call it relaxation because that's the reason people book it. It's the right choice if you want to switch off, not work through knots. For firm pressure, choose deep tissue.

Good to know

  • Light to medium pressure, adjusted to you
  • Full body: back, neck, shoulders, arms, legs and feet
  • Unscented oil on request
  • Tell her about injuries or sunburn and she'll work around them

Questions

Is relaxation massage the same as Swedish massage?

Yes. Spas often call it Swedish massage: long, smooth strokes with light to medium pressure. We call it relaxation massage because that's what people book it for.

Should I book 60 or 90 minutes?

60 minutes covers the full body at an easy pace. 90 minutes gives extra time on your back, neck and shoulders.

How much pressure will my therapist use?

Light to medium, and she'll check with you at the start. If you want firm pressure on knots, book deep tissue instead.

Do I need to shower first?

You don't have to, but rinsing off sand and sunscreen helps the oil glide. Tell your therapist if you have any sunburn so she can avoid it.

Is it OK to fall asleep?

Of course. Many guests do. Your therapist will wake you gently at the end and pack up quietly.
