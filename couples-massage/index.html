Questions

How much space do we need for a couples massage?

Room for two tables side by side, about 3 x 3 meters (10 x 10 feet). A bedroom, living room or covered terrace all work.

Can we each choose our own pressure?

Yes. Each of you has your own therapist, and she adjusts to you. One of you can have it light and the other firmer.

Can a couples massage be a surprise?

Yes. Just message us on WhatsApp, tell us the plan and we'll keep it quiet. We only contact you, not your partner.

What time is best for a sunset couples massage?

Book in the late afternoon and ask for it on the terrace. Sessions run until 8pm, and those after 7pm add $20.

Is the price the same at a hotel?

Yes. $200 for 60 minutes or $300 for 90 minutes, whether you're in a hotel room, villa, Airbnb or on a yacht.
