Small by design

This is a private, high-end group massage. We don't fill your villa with a crowd of therapists. Instead, a small team settles in for a few hours, and your group has massages in turn.

  • It's calmer

    Your therapists stay for the whole session, so each massage gets a full, unhurried hour.

  • It fits more villas

How a group session runs

Here's how a morning or afternoon with us works.

  1. Tell us the headcount

    Send the date, villa, number of people and whether you'd like the morning or the afternoon.

  2. We pick the team

    We send one, two or three therapists, depending on your group and how long you'd like to spend.

  3. Massages in turn

    For example, 6 people with 3 therapists means two rounds, about two and a half hours in total. 9 people with 3 therapists fills a relaxed morning.

  4. One payment at the end

    The group pays one head therapist in cash for everyone.

Questions

How many therapists will come?

One, two or three, depending on your group size and how long you'd like the session to run. We keep it small on purpose, so the villa stays calm.

Can you host more than 9 people?

No. We cap groups at 9 so the session stays relaxed. For bigger groups, St. Maarten Massage Service runs group deals with more therapists at once.

Can we choose male or female therapists?

Yes. Our team includes male and female licensed therapists. Just tell us your preference when you book.

How far ahead should we book a group massage?

We only run a few group sessions, so dates fill up. For groups of 6 or more, a few days ahead is best. Smaller groups can often book the same day.

Do the pastries come with every booking?

They come with morning group sessions, at no extra charge.

Can some people in the group get deep tissue?

Yes. Just tell us when you book. Deep tissue costs $125 for 60 minutes.

How much space does a group massage need?

Each table needs about 2 x 3 meters (6 x 10 feet). Terraces, pool decks and big living rooms all work well.
