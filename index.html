  3. We set up on site

What "onsite" means

You don't go to a spa. The spa is set up where you are. All you need is a little flat space indoors, on a terrace, on a deck or on the sand. We bring:

  • A professional, padded massage table
  • Fresh, pressed sheets and towels for every guest
  • Massage oils, unscented on request
  • Calm music if you'd like it

Your therapist

Our licensed team is mainly female, and a male therapist is available on request. Our therapists have experience working in resorts, spas and private vacation homes.

Your therapist sets up the massage space, works at the spot you picked and leaves it as tidy as she found it. You never have to leave your villa, room or boat.

Our therapists also work with our sister service, SXM Massage Mobile.

Questions

How much is an onsite massage in St. Maarten?

A 60-minute relaxation massage is $100, set up on site wherever you are on the island. 90 minutes is $150. Deep tissue is $120 for 60 minutes and couples massage is $200.

Can I book a massage for today?

Yes. Same-day bookings are welcome. Message us on WhatsApp at +1-721-554-0065 and we'll confirm the earliest open time.

Do I need my own massage table?

No. Your therapist brings a professional table, fresh sheets, towels and oils. You only need a little floor space.

How do I pay?

Cash only, in US dollars, paid to your therapist after the massage. We don't take cards.

Are the therapists female?

The team is mainly female, and a male therapist is available on request.

Can you come to my hotel room?

Yes. We visit hotels and resorts across the island. Please let the front desk know your therapist is coming so they can arrange access.
