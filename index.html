Who we are

Five places to see while you're here

Plan your days around these. Then let us take care of the evenings.

  • Dutch side

    Maho Beach

    Jets land just over the sand at Princess Juliana Airport, a sight people fly in for. Stay out of the marked danger zone and never hold the fence.

    After the jet blast, a relaxation massage resets the whole day.

  • French side

    Orient Bay

    A long white-sand bay with beach clubs, long lunches and water sports. Part of the beach is clothing optional.

    Too much sun? A slow, gentle massage is the perfect way to cool down.

  • French side

    Pinel Island

    A small nature-reserve island a short boat ride off the northeast coast, with calm, clear water for snorkeling.

    Loosen up after a day in the water with a deep tissue massage.

  • French side

    Fort Louis and Marigot

    Climb to the 18th-century fort for the best view over Marigot Bay toward Anguilla, then browse the market on the way down.

    Tired legs from the climb? Ask your therapist to focus on them.

  • French side

    Grand Case

    A small seaside village known as the island's dining capital. Book a table on the water for sunset.

    Start the evening with a couples massage before dinner.

Questions

How much is an onsite massage in St. Maarten?

A 60-minute relaxation massage is $105, and that includes travel. See the full price list above.

How far ahead should I book?

Message us as early as you can, especially for groups, since group dates are limited. For a single massage, same-day booking often works too, but spots are limited in high-traffic areas like Mount Vernon, Anse Marcel and Terres Basses, so book early there to make sure you get your time.

What should I do before my therapist arrives?

Clear a small flat space and have cash ready. If you're in a hotel, let the front desk know a therapist is coming so they can let her up.
