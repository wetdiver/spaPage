Questions

Do I need my host's permission to book a massage?

Usually not, since it's your stay. If you're unsure, check your rental's house rules on visitors. Also, if your building has a front desk or security, let them know a therapist is coming.

Will massage oil get on the rental's furniture or sheets?

No. We bring our own sheets and towels and put a cover down under the table, so the rental's linens and floors stay clean.

What should I send you when I book?

The address or a map pin, the gate code or entrance, where to park, and the unit or floor number.

Can everyone staying in our villa book?

Yes. For 4 or more people, see group massage. It's $88 per person.

I'm a host. Can I recommend you to my guests?

Please do. Download our host card and add it to your welcome book. Then guests book with us directly.
