Simpson Bay: the easy base

Beaches, dinner and groceries, all on foot.

If you're asking us where to stay without a car, Simpson Bay is the answer we give first. Step outside your rental and there's a beach within walking distance in pretty much every direction.

Why it works without a car

Simpson Bay packs restaurants, bars, beaches and everyday shops into one stretch, so you can do almost everything on foot. That's rare on this island. It's also a short ride from the airport, so getting here on day one is easy.

Most nights you'll walk to dinner. A taxi is only for the days you want to explore further.

Beaches you can walk to

Simpson Bay Beach is the long one, and it's the longest beach on the Dutch side. You reach it through the old village behind the busy restaurant strip. It's quieter than you'd expect, given how busy the main road is.

Kim Sha Beach is close by too, and it's a popular spot to rent a kayak.

Pick a different beach each day without ever calling a cab.

Good to know

The Simpson Bay bridge backs up. If you do take a taxi, go out early and come back before 3pm, or wait until after 6pm.

The main road is busy, so walk the side streets and the beach when you can. It's nicer anyway.
