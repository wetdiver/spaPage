How a hotel massage works

It takes a few messages, and many hotel bookings happen the same day.

  • Send us your hotel name and room number on WhatsApp
  • Tell the front desk a therapist is coming
  • Clear about 2 x 3 meters (6 x 10 feet) of floor or balcony
  • Relax. She packs everything away before she leaves

Why book it in your room

No walk through the lobby in a robe, no waiting room and no rush to get back. When your massage ends, you're already in your room, so you can shower, nap or head straight to dinner. Guests often book one after a long flight or a full day in the sun.

Questions

Do I need the hotel's permission for an in-room massage?

Usually not, since it's your room. Just tell the front desk a therapist is coming so they can send her up. Resorts in Maho and other busy areas often route visitors through reception, so a quick call ahead saves a wait in the lobby.

Can you come to my hotel today?

Often, yes, when we have space. Same-day spots are limited, especially in Mount Vernon, Anse Marcel and Terres Basses, where traffic makes the drive longer. Book as early as you can to make sure you get your time. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the earliest opening.

Is there enough space in a hotel room?

One table needs about 2 x 3 meters (6 x 10 feet). Most rooms have that beside the bed or on the balcony. A couples massage needs about 3 x 3 meters (10 x 10 feet). If your room is small, send us a photo on WhatsApp and we'll tell you.

Can we have a couples massage in our hotel room?

Yes. Two therapists bring two tables and set up side by side. It's $210 for 60 minutes, the same as at a villa. More about couples massage.

Does a hotel cost more than a villa?

No. Prices are the same anywhere on the island, and travel is included. Sessions starting after 7pm add $20.

Will massage oil get on the hotel's sheets?

No. We bring our own sheets and towels and put a cover down under the table, so the hotel's linens stay clean.
