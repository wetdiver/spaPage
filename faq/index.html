Prices and payment

How much does a massage cost?

Relaxation massage is $105 for 60 minutes or $160 for 90 minutes. Deep tissue is $125 for 60 minutes. Couples massage is $210 for 60 minutes or $315 for 90 minutes. Group massage is $95 per person for 4 to 9 people. The birthday package is $250.

Is travel included in the price?

Yes. Every price includes travel to you, the table, sheets, towels and oils. Also, there are no booking fees.

How do I pay?

Cash only, in US dollars, paid to your therapist after the massage. We don't take cards or online payments.

How does a group pay?

One cash payment at the end. That way, one head therapist collects for the whole group.

Is there an extra charge for evening massages?

Sessions starting after 7pm add $20.

Treatments

Is relaxation massage the same as Swedish massage?

Yes. It's a gentle, flowing full-body massage with light to medium pressure. We call it relaxation massage because that's what people book it for. More about relaxation massage.

What's the difference between relaxation and deep tissue?

Relaxation massage uses light to medium pressure to help you switch off. Deep tissue, however, uses firm, focused pressure for tight shoulders, backs and legs.

Should I book 60 or 90 minutes?

60 minutes covers the full body at an easy pace. Meanwhile, 90 minutes gives extra time on your back, neck and shoulders.

How does a couples massage work?

Two therapists arrive with two tables and set up side by side, so you both have your massage at the same time. More about couples massage.

What's in the birthday package?

Two therapists and a bottle of champagne for the guest of honor and one friend, for $250.

Can I have unscented oil?

Yes. Ask for unscented oil when you book.

Your therapist

Can I choose a male or female therapist?

Yes. Our team includes male and female licensed therapists. Just tell us your preference when you book.

Are your therapists licensed?

Yes. Our therapists are licensed, with experience in resorts, spas and private vacation homes.

Do I need my own massage table?

No. Your therapist brings a professional table, fresh sheets, towels, oils and music if you'd like it.

Will I stay covered during the massage?

Yes. A sheet covers you throughout, and your therapist uncovers only the area she's working on. So undress only to the level you're comfortable with.

Where we go

Which areas do you cover?

The whole island, Dutch side St. Maarten and French side St. Martin, including Simpson Bay, Maho, Cupecoy, Beacon Hill, Pelican Key, Philipsburg, Pointe Blanche, Dawn Beach, Indigo Bay, Orient Bay, Grand Case and Terres Basses.

Do you come to hotels?

Yes. We come up to your room. Before she arrives, let the front desk know a therapist is on the way.

Do you come to Airbnbs and villa rentals?

Yes. We bring our own sheets and towels and lay a cover under the table, so the rental stays clean. More about Airbnb massage.

Do you come to yachts?

Yes, docked in a marina or at anchor. At anchor, the crew picks your therapist up by tender from an agreed dock. More about yacht massage.

Do you massage yacht crew?

Yes. Four or more crew on the same boat pay the group rate of $95 each.

How much space do you need?

About 2 x 3 meters (6 x 10 feet) for one table, and about 3 x 3 meters (10 x 10 feet) for a couples massage. For example, a terrace, bedroom, living room or yacht deck all work.

Groups and celebrations

Do you do group massages?

Yes, for bachelorettes, birthdays, wedding parties, girls' trips and family reunions. More about group massage.

How many therapists come for a group?

One, two or three. We keep group sessions small on purpose, so the villa stays calm and guests have massages in turn.

How long does a group massage take?

It depends on the group size. For example, 6 people with 3 therapists is two rounds, about two and a half hours in total. 9 people fills a relaxed morning or afternoon.

Can people in a group choose different treatments?

Yes. Tell us who wants deep tissue when you book. Deep tissue is $125 for 60 minutes.

Before your massage

Do I need to shower first?

You don't have to, but rinsing off sand and sunscreen helps. Tell your therapist about any sunburn so she can avoid it.

Should I tell my therapist about injuries?

Yes. Mention injuries, sore spots or areas to avoid at the start and she'll work around them.

Is it OK to fall asleep?

Of course. Many guests do. So relax: your therapist will wake you gently at the end.

How long does setup take?

About 10 minutes, and she packs everything up when she's done.
