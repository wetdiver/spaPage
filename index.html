What we bring

You don't go to a spa. Instead, we bring the spa to you. All you need is a little flat space indoors, on a terrace, on a deck or on the sand. We bring:

  • A professional, padded massage table
  • Fresh, pressed sheets and towels for every guest
  • Massage oils, unscented on request
  • Calm music if you'd like it

Your therapist

Our licensed team is mainly female, and a male therapist is available on request. They also have experience in resorts, spas and private vacation homes.

Five places to see while you're here

Plan your days around these. Then let us take care of the evenings, wherever you're staying.

  • Dutch side

    Maho Beach

    Jets land just over the sand at Princess Juliana Airport, a sight people fly in for. Stay out of the marked danger zone and never hold the fence.

    After the sun and the jet blast, unwind with a relaxation massage back at your villa.

  • French side

    Orient Bay

    A long white-sand bay with beach clubs, long lunches and water sports. Part of the beach is clothing optional.

    End the day with a massage set up on the sand or your terrace.

  • French side

    Pinel Island

    A small nature-reserve island a short boat ride off the northeast coast, with calm, clear water for snorkeling.

    Loosen up after a day in the water with a deep tissue massage.

  • French side

    Fort Louis and Marigot

    Climb to the 18th-century fort for the best view over Marigot Bay toward Anguilla, then browse the market on the way down.

    Rest tired legs with a massage at your rental that evening.

  • French side

    Grand Case

    A small seaside village known as the island's dining capital. Book a table on the water for sunset.

    Start the evening with a couples massage on your terrace before dinner.

Questions

How much is an onsite massage in St. Maarten?

A 60-minute relaxation massage is $105, including travel, set up on site wherever you are on the island. 90 minutes is $160. Deep tissue is $125 for 60 minutes and couples massage is $210.

Can I book a massage for today?

Yes. Same-day bookings are welcome. Message us on WhatsApp at +1-721-554-0065 and then we'll confirm the earliest open time.

Do I need my own massage table?

No. Your therapist brings a professional table, fresh sheets, towels and oils. So you only need a little floor space.

How do I pay?

Cash only, in US dollars, paid to your therapist after the massage. We don't take cards, so please have cash ready.

Are the therapists female?

The team is mainly female, and a male therapist is available on request.

Can you come to my hotel room?

Yes. We visit hotels and resorts across the island. Please let the front desk know your therapist is coming so they can arrange access.
