How a group booking runs

Group massages follow the same simple plan every time.

  1. Tell us the headcount

    Send the date, time, villa and number of people on WhatsApp.

  2. We send therapists in pairs

    Even numbers, so tables run side by side and nobody waits alone at the end.

  3. Massages in rounds

    For example, 8 people with 4 therapists is two rounds, about two and a half hours in total.

  4. One payment at the end

    The group pays one head therapist in cash for everyone.

Questions

How far ahead should we book a group massage?

For groups of 6 or more, a few days ahead is best so we can line up enough therapists. However, smaller groups can often book the same day.

How many therapists will come?

We send therapists in pairs, so two, four or six depending on your group. That way tables run side by side and no one waits alone at the end.

Can some people in the group get deep tissue?

Yes. Just tell us when you book. Deep tissue costs $125 for 60 minutes.

How does the group pay?

One cash payment at the end. The group pays one head therapist for everyone, so nobody has to sort out change table by table.

How much space does a group massage need?

Each table needs about 2 x 3 meters (6 x 10 feet). Terraces, pool decks and big living rooms all work well.
