TheHills: Simpson Bay with a view

One- and two-bedroom apartments on Welfare Road.

We've set up massages at TheHills, including the garden terrace in our photos. It's an easy place to recommend if you'd rather skip the car.

What you get

TheHills Residence rents one- and two-bedroom apartments. Every unit has its own balcony, garden or wall of glass, most looking out over the lagoon.

There's an infinity pool, plus a rooftop jacuzzi on each building with views all around.

Why it works without a car

TheHills is in Simpson Bay, so you're close to everything on our Simpson Bay page. Simpson Bay Beach is a short distance away, and so are the Simpson Bay bridge, restaurants, shops and the casino.

Spend the day at the beach, then come back up for the pool and the sunset.

Good to know

It's at 69 Welfare Road. Book directly through their site, and ask them about parking and kitchen details if those matter to you.

The garden terrace has room for two massage tables side by side, which makes it great for couples.

A massage on your terrace at TheHills

Couples massage on the garden terrace, or a quiet hour on your balcony. We've worked there before and know the setup. We bring the table, fresh linens, oils and towels, and set up in about 10 minutes. Relaxation massage starts at $105 for an hour. Couples start at $210.
