Questions

Do you come to yachts at anchor?

Yes. First, the crew picks your therapist up by tender from a dock we agree on. Then they drop her back when the massage ends.

Where on the boat do you set up?

Anywhere flat with room for the table, about 2 x 3 meters. For example, the aft deck, flybridge or main salon all work.

What if the sea is rough?

Calm water makes for a better massage. If it's choppy at anchor, we'll move the time or meet you at the dock instead.

Do you massage yacht crew?

Yes. Between charters is a popular time. Plus, four or more crew get the group rate of $95 each.

Do you need marina access?

Yes. Let the marina office or security know a therapist is coming, then send us the dock and slip number.
